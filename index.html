Strategy Tips: Binary Search

• Always guess the midpoint of the remaining range.

• Each guess eliminates half of remaining possibilities.

• For range 1–100: you need at most 7 guesses (log₂(100) ≈ 6.6).

• For range 1–1000: at most 10 guesses.

• Efficiency % = (optimal guesses / your guesses) × 100
